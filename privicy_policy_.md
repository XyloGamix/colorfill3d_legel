## 3. Information We Collect and Process

### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
When you play **Color Fill 3D**, certain game data is generated and saved locally on your device (using standard browser LocalStorage and Android SharedPreferences):
- **Level Progress:** Completed stages, unlocked infinite procedural stages (e.g., levels 1 to 10,000+), and stars acquired.
- **Player Preferences:** Sound effects toggle (ON/OFF), Virtual D-Pad control toggle (ON/OFF), and selected solid visual color themes.
- **High Scores & Statistics:** Highest level cleared, total moves made, cubes cleared, and win/loss records.
- **Cached Configuration:** Offline copy of game parameters and ad display rules.

*Note: This data remains on your device and can be erased at any time by clearing the Game's cache/data via your device settings or by uninstalling the application.*

### B. Automatically Collected Technical Data & Identifiers
When the Game connects to our online services (such as leaderboards and dynamic configuration), the following non-personally identifiable technical information is processed:
- **Random Anonymous Device ID:** A randomly generated alphanumeric identifier (e.g., `dev_XXXX_YYYY`) created upon first launch and stored locally. This is not tied to your personal identity, SIM card, or Google account.
- **Anonymous Player Nickname:** An auto-assigned default nickname (e.g., `Painter #9E4F`) which you may customize inside the game.
- **Hardware & Software Metadata:** Operating system type (`Android` or `Web`), basic platform identifier, and application version (e.g., `1.0.0`).
- **Session Metrics:** Count of game sessions initiated and timestamp of last activity.

### C. Advertising & Telemetry Data (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob)
To keep **Color Fill 3D** free to play, we serve mobile advertisements via **Google AdMob**. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device data, including:
- **Google Advertising ID (AAID):** A resettable device identifier used by Google to serve relevant advertisements, prevent ad fraud, enforce frequency capping, and measure ad performance.
- **Ad Interaction Data:** Information regarding impressions, clicks, views of rewarded videos, and ad delivery status.
- **IP Address & Coarse Location:** Approximate geographic location derived from IP address (country or city level) used for regional ad compliance and language serving.
- **Diagnostic Information:** Crash logs, network latency, and ad loading errors.

### D. Information You Provide Directly to Us
- **Support Inquiries:** If you contact us directly via email at **[allgames9e@gmail.com](mailto:allgames9e@gmail.com)** for technical assistance, bug reports, or feedback, we will receive your email address and any text or attachments you choose to provide. We use this information solely to answer your questions and resolve your technical issues.

### E. Information We NEVER Collect
We strictly respect user boundaries. We do **NOT** request, collect, or store:
- Real names, home addresses, or phone numbers

- Contacts, address books, call logs, or SMS

## 6. Android Permissions Requested

**Color Fill 3D** asks only for the bare minimum permissions required for networking and ad display:

| Permission | Technical Code | Justification & Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Internet Access** | `android.permission.INTERNET` | Required to communicate with Google AdMob servers for ad loading and with Supabase for leaderboard synchronizations and remote configurations. |
| **Network State** | `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Allows the game to verify whether an active Wi-Fi or cellular connection exists before attempting network calls, avoiding battery and data waste. |
| **Advertising ID** | `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` | Standard Google Play permission enabling the Google Mobile Ads SDK to read the Google Advertising ID for ad serving, compliance, and fraud protection. |

*No high-risk or sensitive runtime permissions (such as `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `CAMERA`, or `RECORD_AUDIO`) are requested.*
